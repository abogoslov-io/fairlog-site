Fairlog for Jira Cloud · Last updated: 24 August 2026

Fairlog is operated by PE Anton Bogoslov Igor (Individual Entrepreneur, Armenia).

The short version

Fairlog runs entirely on Atlassian infrastructure (Forge). Your data never leaves Atlassian: the app has no servers of its own, makes no external network calls, and sends nothing to third parties. We — the vendor — have no direct access to your data.

What Fairlog processes

  • User identifiers — Atlassian accountId, display name, avatar link — of the people who log work in your Jira instance.
  • Work time — Jira worklogs (date, duration, issue, author) and their billable/client marks.
  • Billing configuration of your organisation — clients (accounts), rates (including rates tied to a specific person, which are personal data), billing statement lines and totals.

No special categories of data are processed. No profiling, no advertising, no model training.

Where it is stored

All data lives in Atlassian Forge hosted storage (Key-Value Store and Forge SQL) tied to your Jira instance. A cache of worklog data (including accountIds) is kept for the app's reporting features for as long as the app is installed. If your Atlassian product is pinned to a data-residency region, Forge keeps the app's data in the same region automatically. Atlassian Pty Ltd is the only subprocessor.

Who can see what

Rates and amounts are visible to the app's admin/finance roles only; managers see team hours; members see their own hours. These restrictions are enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI.

Your rights & data removal

  • Access, correction or deletion/anonymisation requests for a specific user are fulfilled via our support channel within 30 days.
  • On deletion, we remove the user's cached worklog rows, personal rates and app role; lines of already-issued billing statements are anonymised ("removed user"), as they are financial records of your organisation.
  • If you uninstall Fairlog, Atlassian retains Forge hosted storage for 28 days and then deletes it. Worklogs themselves are native Jira data and stay in your Jira.

Diagnostics

We rely on Forge platform logs for diagnostics. Logs contain errors and technical identifiers only — never the content of your worklogs, rates or statements.

Billing

Billing, refunds and licensing are handled by Atlassian Marketplace. We never see your payment details.

This website
