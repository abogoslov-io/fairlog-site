Support

Fairlog — Client Billing & Reports on Native Worklogs

Response targets

  • Critical (app inoperable, incorrect statement data): first response — target 1 business day.
  • Everything else: within 5 business days.

How to reach us

  • Open an issue in our public tracker: github.com/abogoslov-io/fairlog-site/issues
  • Or use the support link on the Atlassian Marketplace listing (goes live with the listing).

Before you write

For billing-figure questions, please include: the statement number, the period, and whether the Changes screen shows drift for it. For sync questions: the "Sync" tab status line. Never include passwords or API tokens.

Data subject requests (GDPR)

Deletion/anonymisation requests per accountId are handled within 30 days — see the DPA.

Incidents

Security or availability incidents affecting customers: we notify Atlassian within 48 hours and publish a notice here.
