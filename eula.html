1. License

Fairlog is licensed on a non-exclusive, non-transferable basis for the duration of an active Atlassian Marketplace subscription. The free tier (up to 10 site users) is provided "as is" under the same terms. Billing, refunds and licensing are handled by Atlassian Marketplace.

2. Data

Fairlog runs exclusively on Atlassian infrastructure (Forge) and does not transmit customer data to any external servers (no egress). Worklogs remain native Jira objects owned by the customer. App configuration (accounts, rates, statements, cache) is stored in Forge hosted storage within the customer's instance and is exportable to CSV. Personal data processing is governed by the DPA.

3. Platform dependency and Forge quotas

Fairlog depends on the availability, limits and quotas of the Atlassian Forge platform, which the Vendor does not control. If Forge platform quotas or platform incidents apply, some functions (sync, report and statement generation, background jobs) may be delayed or temporarily unavailable. The Vendor designs within quotas and monitors consumption, but cannot guarantee operation during platform limitations or Atlassian incidents. Material platform changes affecting functionality are reflected in release notes.

4. Support

Support channel is listed on the Marketplace listing. Critical issues (app inoperable, incorrect statement data): first response — target 1 business day. Other requests: within 5 business days. Security or availability incidents affecting customers are reported to Atlassian within 48 hours, with a customer notice published.

5. Disclaimer

Fairlog produces billing statements / invoice-ready reports — supporting documents for billing. It does not produce tax invoices and does not guarantee tax compliance in any jurisdiction. Calculations are based on Jira worklogs and customer-entered rates; customers review statements before sending them (drift alerts and review tooling are provided for this purpose).

6. Liability

The Vendor's aggregate liability is limited to the amounts paid for the App in the 12 months preceding the event. Indirect damages are excluded to the maximum extent permitted by law.

7. Term and termination

This agreement applies while the subscription is active. Upon uninstallation, Forge hosted storage data is retained by Atlassian for 28 days and then deleted per Forge platform policies. Customers are advised to export rates, accounts and statements (CSV) before uninstalling.
